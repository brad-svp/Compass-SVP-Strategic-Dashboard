SVP Compass

True North mark: one circle, one two-part needle pointing northeast. The blue half is direction; the navy half is the ground we stand on. Internal staff workspace for SVP Sacramento.

SVP Compass is a product mark for the staff workspace. It does not replace the SVP Sacramento organizational logo.
