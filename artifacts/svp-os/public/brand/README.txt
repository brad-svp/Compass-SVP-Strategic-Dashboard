SVP COMPASS — TRUE NORTH ASSET PACKAGE

This identity belongs to the internal SVP Sacramento staff workspace.
It does not replace Social Venture Partners Sacramento's corporate identity.

SVG
- svp-compass-mark: standalone primary compass
- svp-compass-lockup: horizontal mark and outlined wordmark
- *-dark: reversed colors for navy backgrounds (transparent canvas)
- *-mono-navy / *-mono-cream: single-color variants
- svp-compass-app-icon: contained icon for favicons and touch icons

PNG
Transparent mark and wordmark exports: @1x, @2x, @3x.
App icons intentionally include a colored tile; outside corners are transparent.
Wordmark SVG text is outlined; recipients do not need fonts installed.

FAVICONS
16px, 32px and 48px PNG; multi-size ICO; 180px Apple touch icon.

GUIDE AND TOKENS
